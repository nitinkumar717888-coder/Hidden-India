## 2. Shot Composition & Visual Requirements (Batch 1 Focus)

### Destination 1: Bhima Devi Temple Complex (Panchkula / Pinjore, Haryana)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Wide frontal/axial perspective of the excavated Panchayatana sandstone temple plinth, showing the main sanctum layout and ruined foundations in clear daylight.
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Distinctive 8th–12th century Gurjara-Pratihara stone carvings (e.g. Mahishasuramardini, celestial musicians, or carved miniature shikhara fragments in the sculpture pavilion).
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Landscaped archaeological garden walkway with background profile of the Shiwalik foothills.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Excavated stone plinth and foundations of the medieval Bhima Devi Temple Complex in Pinjore"`

### Destination 2: Bassi Baoli (Pinjore Stepwell, Haryana)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Downward viewpoint looking into the descending stone stairs and subterranean arched well shaft leading to the spring-fed water level.
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Close-up of dressed ashlar masonry courses, spring intake conduits, and carved stone lamp alcoves.
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Village approach road off the Pinjore-Kalka corridor showing the immediate settlement fabric bordering the baoli structure.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Descending stone flight of stairs and arched well shaft at Bassi Baoli in Pinjore"`

### Destination 3: Burail Fort Bastion (Sector 45, Chandigarh)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Full vertical exterior elevation of the 40-foot round lakhori-brick bastion viewed from street level.
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Distinctive narrow lakhori brick masonry courses, lime-surkhi bonding mortar, and defensive musket loop slits.
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Urban juxtaposition of the historic fortification bastion standing amid modern residential buildings and local market stalls of Sector 45-C.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Surviving round brick bastion of Burail Fort standing amid the streetscape of Sector 45 in Chandigarh"`

### Destination 4: Sheikh Chehli’s Tomb & Harsh Ka Tila (Kurukshetra, Haryana)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Clear daylight elevation of the octagonal white marble tomb dome set upon the elevated red sandstone terrace.
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Intricate pierced marble jali lattice screens, carved sandstone floral pilasters, or the exposed archaeological stratification at adjoining Harsh Ka Tila.
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Madrasa arcades, Charbagh quadrant courtyard, and paved visitor approach.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Octagonal white marble dome of Sheikh Chehli's Tomb upon its red sandstone terrace in Kurukshetra"`

### Destination 5: Aam Khas Bagh (Sirhind / Fatehgarh Sahib, Punjab)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Panoramic view across the historic garden terraces toward the Mughal royal pavilions (Daulat Khana-e-Khas).
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Vaulted subterranean cooling chambers (Sard Khana), terracotta water conduit pipes, or hamam brick masonry vaults.
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Monumental perimeter lakhori brick gateway opening off the historic Grand Trunk Road route.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Arched Mughal garden pavilions and water terraces at Aam Khas Bagh in Sirhind"`

### Destination 6: Mughal Sarai Doraha (Doraha / Ludhiana, Punjab)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Monumental double-storey northern or southern gateway flanked by octagonal brick bastions and central arched portal.
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Surviving Kashikari glazed pottery tiles (turquoise, yellow, and cobalt) embedded in the gateway spandrels.
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Expansive interior quadrangle showing perimeter traveler rooms, ruined well, and adjoining agricultural fields.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Monumental brick entrance gateway of the 17th-century Mughal Sarai at Doraha"`

### Destination 7: Jal Mahal (Narnaul / Mahendragarh, Haryana)
* **Required Photo 1 — Hero:** Symmetrical daylight perspective of the square pleasure palace floating in the center of the Khan Sarovar water basin, viewed from the northern approach.
* **Required Photo 2 — Detail:** Persian chronogram inscription above the gateway (AH 999 / 1591 CE), corner chhatris, or water sluice gates.
* **Optional Photo 3 — Context:** Multi-arched stone causeway bridge connecting the northern bank to the palace pavilion.
* **Alt-Text Standard:** `"Square water palace pavilion in the center of Khan Sarovar reservoir at Jal Mahal in Narnaul"`

## 3. Strict Provenance & Intake Standard

For every authentic replacement photograph submitted for publication:
1. **Photographer Attribution:** Legal photographer name recorded; first-party shoots attributed as `"Photo: Hidden India"`.
2. **Capture Date:** YYYY-MM-DD format recorded.
3. **Source / Provenance:** Field log ID, survey repository link, or verified open-access archive URI.
4. **License / Legal Permission:** Must be either Direct First-Party Copyright (Hidden India team) or documented open license (e.g. CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, Public Domain).
5. **Editorial Caption Standard:** Captions must describe only visible physical elements. Unsupported historical assertions or legends must not be introduced into image captions.
6. **No AI / No Scraped Stock:** AI-generated images, scraped social media photographs, and Google Maps screenshots are strictly barred from intake.
7. **Flag Transition:** `requiresEditorialReplacement: true` must remain active on every destination until all required photos (Hero + Detail) are verified and reviewed by the editorial lead.
