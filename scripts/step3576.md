# Hidden India — 30-Destination Expansion Batch Implementation Plan

## Objective
Expand the Hidden India destination dataset from 22 researched destinations to 52 total destination records (adding 30 expansion candidates across Delhi, Rajasthan, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, and Punjab).
The 22 core published destinations remain intact, unchanged, and preserved. All 30 new destinations will be added with full structured metadata, authoritative sources, verified coordinates, evidence classifications, and will be set to `editorialStatus: 'draft'` so they are not publicly exposed until editorial verification and authentic photography standards are satisfied.

---

## User Review Required

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Dataset Architecture & Test Compatibility**
> 1. **Total Dataset Count**: The total dataset in `RESEARCHED_DESTINATIONS` will expand from 22 to 52 destinations.
> 2. **Editorial Publication Gate**: The existing 22 destinations will remain `editorialStatus: 'published'`. All 30 newly introduced destinations will have `editorialStatus: 'draft'`, meaning they will be visible in the Admin CMS and draft preview mode (`?preview=true`), but excluded from the public sitemap, search, and public catalog until positive editorial and photographic verification.
> 3. **Preservation of Existing 22 Alias**: We will export `RESEARCHED_22_DESTINATIONS` (representing the 22 published core destinations) and update existing historical test assertions so that tests specifically verifying the 22-destination published core assert on `RESEARCHED_22_DESTINATIONS` (or `RESEARCHED_DESTINATIONS.filter(d => d.editorialStatus === 'published')`), while new tests assert on the complete 52-record catalog.
